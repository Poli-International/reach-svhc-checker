REACH SVHC Pigment Checker

Flag Substances of Very High Concern in tattoo inks, body jewelry alloys, and studio products against the ECHA Candidate List ( · substances).

Accepts ingredient names, aliases, CAS numbers, or EC numbers. Covers metals, PAHs, phthalates, aromatic amines, and other SVHC categories relevant to body art.

The scanner extracts CAS numbers (e.g. 7440-02-0) and ingredient names, then checks each against the SVHC list. Works with INCI lists, SDS sections, and raw ingredient blocks.
